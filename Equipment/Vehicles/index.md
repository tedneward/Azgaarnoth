## [Sea](./Sea/index.md)
Any travel over sea requires specialized knowlege.

### Vehicle (Water) Proficiency
Checks with this tool proficiency add the PC's proficiency bonus if they have **Navigator's Tools** when they make it.

-   **Strength (Athletics or raw Strength):** Handling heavy rigging, hoisting sails, or rowing.
-   **Dexterity:** Swiftly running the lines, tying knots, or navigating treacherous rigging during a storm.
-   **Wisdom (Survival or Perception):** Reading the weather, spotting reefs, or steering the helm safely.
-   **Intelligence:** Charting navigation maps or calculating travel pace with Navigator's Tools.
